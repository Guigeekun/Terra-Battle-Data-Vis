Welcome to the Terra Battle Visualizer

This workspace extracts the internal MonoBehaviour data and text resources from the Terra Battle APK and maps them against downloaded game assets. Use this dashboard to search characters, view stats/jobs, browse chapters, read Lua scripts, and play background music and sound effects directly in the browser.
